About the Firm

Strategic Counsel. Elevated Advocacy.

Bannister Law Firm provides refined legal representation for individuals, businesses, associations, and insurers throughout Fort Lauderdale, Broward County, and South Florida.

Founder

Brenisha A. Bannister-Wesley, Esq.

Brenisha A. Bannister-Wesley is the founder of Bannister Law Firm and a strategic, results-driven attorney with a sophisticated background in insurance litigation, personal injury, property damage, landlord-tenant, and community association law.

She is admitted to practice in the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida and is known for delivering high-level legal representation with precision, efficiency, and discretion.

Bar Admissions: Southern District of Florida and Middle District of Florida.

Experience: Insurance litigation, personal injury, property damage, landlord-tenant matters, and community association law.

Perspective: Representation informed by work on both plaintiff and defense-side matters.

Mission

A Higher Standard of Representation

Bannister Law Firm was founded with a clear vision: to deliver a more refined, client-centered legal experience rooted in autonomy, accountability, and results. Brenisha built her practice to move beyond the constraints of traditional law firm structures, offering clients direct access, thoughtful strategy, and a level of service defined by responsiveness, precision, and intentional advocacy.

Her dual-perspective background includes representing insurance carriers and corporate defendants in high-volume defense practices, while also advocating for plaintiffs in insurance subrogation and community association matters. This experience allows the firm to anticipate opposing strategies, identify leverage points early, and position clients for meaningful outcomes.
